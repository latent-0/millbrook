## Problem

**Buyers shortlist inside an AI answer now. Most companies cannot see theirs.**

A buyer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews who to consider, and the answer names a handful. That list is the new shortlist, and it forms before anyone opens your site.

You cannot manage what you cannot see, and AI visibility is almost impossible to see. Teams fall back on a single screenshot of one chat, a gut feel, or an agency dashboard that reports a score and stops there. Nobody can tell you how often you are actually named, which competitors the model prefers, or what to build to change it.

There is a measured way.

## What Cailyx does (benefit-led; replaces the 4 blocks)

**Eyebrow:** Capabilities
**Section H2:** One engine for the whole AI-visibility loop.

- **Make sure the AI can reach you.**
  Cailyx fetches your site the way crawlers do, probes robots.txt and the CDN with real AI user-agents, and flags the silent blocks and JavaScript-render walls that turn an assistant away before it ever reads a word.

- **Give models a name they can resolve.**
  It audits your structured data, checks your entity across the web, and disambiguates you from a same-named company, so an assistant knows it is reading you and not your rival.

- **Measure how you show up, as a rate.**
  Against a query set you own, Cailyx runs each prompt across the answer engines five or more times and reports your mention rate, citation rate, and share of voice. A distribution, not one lucky answer.

- **Show the shortlist you are missing.**
  It names the competitors the models recommend in your category and where they beat you, so you know exactly which answers and which lists to win.

- **Build the fix, not just the finding.**
  Agentic workflows draft the answer-shaped content, the internal and third-party links, and the structured entities that raise citation share. Rothenhall reviews and ships it, so the work gets done, not just reported.

- **Simulate the buyer before the buyer does.**
  Cailyx runs synthetic personas through realistic journeys and a panel of review agents stress-tests the plan, so you find the gaps in the answer before a customer does.

- **Track the score over time and alert you.**
  Your AI Visibility Score, its five dimensions, and your share of voice sit on a monitored trend. When it moves, up or down, Cailyx tells you.

## How it works (the pipeline)

**Eyebrow:** How it runs
**H2:** From invisible to measured to moved, in five steps.

Intake → Measure → Diagnose → Build → Report → Watch
1. **Intake.** You set the category, the competitors, and the buyer questions that matter.
2. **Measure.** Cailyx samples every engine, repeatedly, and records how you are described, cited, and ranked.
3. **Diagnose.** It rolls the evidence into an AI Visibility Score and the specific, reproducible reasons behind it.
4. **Build.** Agents and a Rothenhall operator produce the fixes: schema, content, citations, links.
5. **Watch.** Re-runs track the shift, so growth is provable and nothing quietly regresses.

## The measurement standard (the credibility callout)

**Eyebrow:** Why the number holds up
**H2:** Measured like research, not like a screenshot.

- Five or more runs per prompt, across two or more geographies, so one answer is never mistaken for a trend.
- A named, versioned query set that you own, not a preset list.
- Distributions and share of voice, never a claimed position or rank.
- Every score links back to the runs and checks that produced it.
- AI answers are probabilistic. Cailyx reports likelihood, not promises.

One line: A visibility tool you cannot reproduce is a mood. Cailyx ships the method to reproduce every claim.
