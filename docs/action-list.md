## Phase 1, Proof (first 90 days)

- [ ] Sign a small founding cohort (2 to 4) of startups and/or portfolio
      companies at reduced or fixed fee in exchange for documented results.
- [ ] Run the standard engagement arc on each: diagnose, build, operate,
      compound.
- [ ] Capture baselines on day one (citation share, pipeline, conversion, RevOps
      data state) before any change.
- [ ] Produce the first 2 to 3 before-and-after case studies with real numbers.
- [ ] Seed the proprietary library: first citation-tracking dataset, first
      RevOps diagnostic, first playbook.

## Phase 2, Demand (months 3 to 9)

- [ ] Publish the research findings widely (LinkedIn, direct-to-fund, founder
      communities). The research is the top-of-funnel magnet.
- [ ] Direct outreach to target funds and founders, led with a specific insight
      from a case study or the research, never a generic pitch.
- [ ] Turn each case study into a distribution asset (teardown, short post,
      one-pager for funds).
- [ ] Build a referral loop: every completed engagement asks for one warm intro
      to a peer company or the fund.
- [ ] Establish a repeatable "diagnostic" as a low-friction first offer (the
      audit that opens the retainer).

## Phase 3, Scale (months 9+)

- [ ] Convert individual portfolio-company wins into a **portfolio-wide retainer**
      pitch to the fund.
- [ ] Productize the playbook library so a new engagement starts further ahead
      each time (the moat compounding into faster delivery).
- [ ] Decide the first hire or fractional bench (RevOps or growth operator) to
      lift the delivery ceiling without diluting the single-owner promise.
- [ ] Formalize pricing tiers now that fixed-fee sprints, retainers, and
      portfolio retainers each have proof behind them.

## Standing principles (do not drift from these)

- One accountable owner per engagement. No hand-offs.
- Own the full stack, always sold as one system, never as point services.
- Market with documented proof, not paid ads.
- Feed every engagement back into the library. The compounding is the business.
