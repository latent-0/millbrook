# Rothenhall Partners, Off-Site SEO + AEO Playbook

The on-site work is done (structured entity, answer-shaped pages, sitemap,
robots, canonical/OG, FAQ). What decides whether AI engines and Google actually
recommend you is off-site: the signals across the web that let a model bind
"Rothenhall" to AEO/GEO/GTM/RevOps with confidence. A live ChatGPT test proved
this: it failed to recognise the entity, not the capability. This playbook fixes
that.

Rule zero: **be consistent.** Use the exact name, address, and phone (NAP)
everywhere, verbatim.

```
Name:    Rothenhall Partners
Address: 2nd Floor, HAL 2nd Stage, Vimanapura S.O., Bengaluru 560017, Karnataka, India
Phone:   +91 93983 86765
Email:   office@rothenhall.com
Site:    https://www.rothenhall.com
```

---

## Phase 1, Entity foundation (do first, week 1)

These are the profiles an AI checks to answer "who is this and what do they do".
Create them, fill them completely, and use identical NAP + the same one-line
description ("Fractional operating partner for AI-era growth: AEO/GEO, GTM, and
RevOps as one accountable engine, India and worldwide").

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** (business.google.com). Category: Marketing
      agency / Business management consultant. Verify the Bengaluru address. This
      is the biggest single local-SEO signal for India.
- [ ] **LinkedIn Company Page** + fully built **founder profile(s)** linking to
      the company. Post consistently (see Phase 4).
- [ ] **Crunchbase** company profile (industries: Marketing, AI, RevOps).
- [ ] **Wikidata item** for Rothenhall Partners (organisation, instance of
      "business"/"consulting firm", with official website and the disambiguating
      description). Wikidata is easier than Wikipedia and directly feeds entity
      graphs and disambiguation.
- [ ] Once these exist, **send me the URLs** and I will wire them into the site's
      `sameAs` structured data so the schema points at them.

## Phase 2, Directories and category listings (weeks 1 to 3)

Get listed where buyers and, crucially, LLM training/citation corpora look.

- [ ] Agency directories: **Clutch, G2, DesignRush, Sortlist, GoodFirms,
      The Manifest, UpCity**. Pick categories: SEO, digital marketing, marketing
      strategy, RevOps.
- [ ] India-specific: relevant Indian startup/agency directories and ecosystem
      lists (YourStory partner lists, Inc42, local B2B directories).
- [ ] Ensure every listing carries identical NAP and the same description.

## Phase 3, Reviews and social proof (weeks 2 to 8)

AI answers weight third-party validation heavily.

- [ ] Collect **Clutch and G2 reviews** from your founding-cohort clients (aim
      for 5+ on at least one platform to trigger rich display).
- [ ] Google Business Profile reviews.
- [ ] LinkedIn recommendations for the founder.

## Phase 4, Content distribution and digital PR (ongoing)

This is what feeds LLM "memory" over time. The goal is many credible pages that
repeat: Rothenhall -> AEO/GEO -> GTM -> RevOps -> B2B -> India.

- [ ] **Republish and pitch the /research study.** It is your strongest citable
      asset. Post the findings on LinkedIn, pitch it to Inc42, YourStory,
      newsletters, and AI/marketing communities.
- [ ] **Founder LinkedIn cadence:** 2 to 3 posts/week on AEO/GEO, each reinforcing
      the entity and linking back to a site guide.
- [ ] **Guest posts** on marketing/SaaS/RevOps publications with an author bio
      linking to rothenhall.com.
- [ ] **Podcasts** (Indian SaaS/GTM shows and global marketing shows).
- [ ] **HARO / Qwoted / Featured** to earn press mentions and quotes.
- [ ] Answer relevant questions on **Reddit, Quora, and community Slacks/Discords**
      where AEO/GEO/RevOps come up (helpful, not spammy).

## Phase 5, Backlinks (ongoing, quality over quantity)

Follow Google's guidance: earn links from relevant, trustworthy sites with
descriptive anchor text. Never buy links or join link schemes.

- [ ] Partner pages (tools you use: CRM, analytics vendors).
- [ ] Client sites ("growth partner: Rothenhall Partners").
- [ ] Resource/roundup pages in the AEO/GEO/RevOps space.
- [ ] Anchor text should describe the destination ("fractional AEO and RevOps
      partner"), not "click here".

## Phase 6, Win the listicles LLMs cite (weeks 3 to 12)

When someone asks "best AEO agencies" or "GEO agencies in India", the model
pulls from listicles. Get into them.

- [ ] Search the exact benchmark prompts (below), note which articles/directories
      the engines cite, then pitch those authors/publishers for inclusion.
- [ ] Target lists: "best AEO agencies", "best GEO agencies", "top B2B SaaS
      marketing agencies India", "fractional GTM/RevOps firms".

## Measurement, run monthly

Across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, run these and record
for each: named? position? description? cited? source cited? competitor mentions?
category assigned? recommended for what use case?

1. Best AEO/GEO agencies for B2B SaaS
2. Best GTM + RevOps agencies for early-stage B2B SaaS
3. Agency that can own GEO/AEO and RevOps together
4. Best AI-search agency for an Indian B2B startup
5. Rothenhall alternatives
6. Who competes with Rothenhall?

Also: submit `sitemap.xml` in **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster
Tools** (Bing feeds ChatGPT and Copilot), and watch impressions/clicks and
indexed pages.

## Do / Don't (from Google's SEO starter guide)

**Do:** original people-first content, descriptive titles + anchor text, one
canonical per page, promote via social and word of mouth, keep NAP consistent,
link to trustworthy sources.

**Don't:** stuff keywords, copy content, buy links or use link schemes, rely on
the meta keywords tag (Google ignores it), use intrusive interstitials, or obsess
over word count and domain keywords.
