# Rothenhall Partners, Strategy

_Fractional operating partner for AI-era growth._

## 1. The thesis

Buyers no longer search and scroll to find vendors. They ask an answer engine,
and ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews decide which handful of
companies are worth naming. The AI boom created this bottleneck. It did not solve
it.

Most venture- and PE-backed companies, and most early-stage startups, have no
strategy for being found or cited in those answers. And even when demand does
arrive, the revenue infrastructure behind it is fragmented and manual, because
these companies are lean by design and rarely carry in-house marketing or RevOps
depth.

So they do one of two things:

1. Nothing, and slowly lose visibility to competitors already appearing in AI
   answers.
2. Bolt together disconnected point solutions (a legacy SEO agency with no grasp
   of citation mechanics, a separate RevOps consultant, a fractional CMO) that
   never coordinate, producing duplicated spend and no single owner of the
   outcome.

Rothenhall exists to be that single owner.

## 2. What we do

One accountable operator across the full revenue stack:

| Discipline | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Visibility (AEO / GEO)** | Being named and cited by answer engines. Content, entities, and citations engineered to how models actually select sources. |
| **Go-to-Market** | Positioning, messaging, and launch. The sharp story and the operating cadence that turn a category insight into pipeline. |
| **Revenue Operations** | CRM, attribution, and reporting so growth is measurable and every citation ties back to revenue. |
| **Growth Operating** | The running acquisition and conversion engine, owned and iterated week over week. |

The value is not any single discipline. It is that one operator runs them as one
system: AI visibility without RevOps is unmeasurable, and RevOps without a growth
engine has nothing to measure.

## 4. Competitive landscape

**Direct competitors**

- GEO / AEO specialist agencies (e.g. Citable, Rivetline): move citations, but
  hand the pipeline to someone else.
- RevOps and fractional GTM firms serving PE portfolios (e.g. JayOh, FullFunnel,
  RevPartners): fix the plumbing, without owning whether AI ever names you.
- PE-focused fractional CMO firms (e.g. Chief Outsiders): senior marketing
  leadership, rarely the AI-citation and RevOps depth.

None combine AI-visibility work with RevOps and GTM execution under one
accountable owner.

**Indirect competitors**

- Legacy SEO agencies that have not adapted to AI search.
- Generalist fractional CMO marketplaces.
- Large consultancies (Bain, McKinsey) running GTM practices for funds.
- The default: do nothing, or hire a single full-time marketing lead who cannot
  cover AEO, GTM, and RevOps at once.

## 5. The moat

Full-stack ownership compounds. Every engagement adds to a proprietary library:

- **Citation-tracking data** on how answer engines actually behave.
- **RevOps diagnostics** from real revenue systems.
- **Portfolio playbooks** refined against outcomes.

It is not patentable, but it functions like intellectual property. It cannot be
reproduced by reading a blog post or hiring a single specialist, and it is why
the second engagement is sharper than the first, and the tenth sharper still.
This library is the closest thing the venture has to a defensible asset.

## 6. Proof over claims

Credibility with funds and founders is earned through demonstrated results, not
paid advertising. Marketing leans entirely on documented before-and-after case
studies. Every engagement is captured with the same structure: baseline,
intervention, outcome, attribution.

The research asset (the `/research` analysis of how Claude decides who to
recommend, built on roughly 90,000 answers across 15+ industries) is the
top-of-funnel credibility engine: it demonstrates the citation-mechanics
expertise before a single sales conversation.

- AI citation share across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews for target
  queries.
- Qualified pipeline sourced and influenced.
- Conversion rate through a funnel measured end to end.
- Reporting integrity: a single source of truth a fund can trust.
- Internal: number of documented case studies, and reuse of playbook assets per
  new engagement (the moat compounding).
