## Problem

**Buyers no longer scroll. They ask.**

They ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, and the answer names a handful of companies. That list is the new shortlist, and it is built before anyone reaches your website.

Most venture- and PE-backed companies have no strategy for being in it. So they do one of two things. They wait, and slowly lose discovery to competitors the model already repeats. Or they bolt together a legacy SEO agency that does not know citation mechanics, a separate RevOps consultant, and a fractional CMO who never coordinate, which buys three roadmaps and no single owner of the outcome.

The cost of doing nothing is quiet. You cannot see the answers you are missing. There is a better way.

## Objections, handled

**Can you guarantee we will show up in ChatGPT?**
No, and no one who promises that is telling you the truth. AI answers are probabilistic. We move your rates across a defined query set and report the change honestly. That is the only defensible offer.

**We already have an SEO agency.**
Keep them. Ranking and being recommended are now two different problems, and classic SEO only reaches the first. We own the AI-answer side and the RevOps behind it, and work alongside your existing team.

**How long until we see movement?**
Infrastructure and early visibility gains show in weeks. Compounding citation share and pipeline build across the engagement. We baseline on day one so every change is measured, not claimed.

**We are pre-revenue and lean. Is it too early?**
A fixed-fee sprint is built for exactly this. A defined, time-boxed push that lays the foundation without the cost or risk of a full hire.
