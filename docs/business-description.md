## Long (about 220 words, for a full About page or press kit)

Rothenhall Partners is a fractional operating partner practice for the AI era,
based in Bengaluru and working with founders and funds worldwide.

The way companies get discovered has changed. Buyers increasingly ask an AI answer
engine instead of searching and scrolling, and ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI
Overviews decide which handful of companies are worth naming. Most venture- and
PE-backed companies, and most early-stage startups, have no strategy for being
found or cited there. And even when demand arrives, the revenue infrastructure
behind it is fragmented and manual, because these teams are lean by design.

Rothenhall solves both as a single accountable operator across four disciplines:
AI answer-engine visibility (AEO and GEO), go-to-market, revenue operations, and
growth operating. Instead of stitching together a legacy SEO agency, a separate
RevOps consultant, and a fractional CMO who never coordinate, one operator owns
the whole revenue stack and is accountable for the outcome.

Engagements come in three forms: fixed-fee sprints for early-stage startups,
operating retainers for individual portfolio companies, and portfolio-wide
retainers for funds. Every engagement is documented as a rigorous
before-and-after, because credibility here is earned through proof, not claims.
